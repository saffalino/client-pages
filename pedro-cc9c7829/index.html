Part 4 · The Ideas

A mix: faith-clear titles to own the niche, broad titles to grow it

Faith titles say who the channel is for. Broad titles reach further, with the faith in the thumbnail and the content.

How long

Make them 12 minutes or longer, made for YouTube

We compared every long-form video against its own channel's usual video: 5,183 videos from 175 money and business channels in the last 12 months.

You already hold attention

On your challenge replays, people watch 16 to 31 minutes per view. Long videos suit you.

Made for YouTube

Your replays run 60 to 100 minutes and cover a whole session. Videos built for YouTube get to the point in the first minute and keep one idea all the way through.
