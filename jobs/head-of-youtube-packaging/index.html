About Talking Heads

Talking Heads is a done-for-you YouTube team for established founders and experts. We handle everything from strategy, titles and thumbnails to editing, animation and A/B testing.

We work with channels like Ali Abdaal, Daniel Priestley and Vishen Lakhiani. Our founder, Saf, was previously Creative Director for Ali Abdaal.

The business is already oversubscribed from referrals alone.

Our dream roster includes top creators like Colin and Samir, top companies like Formula 1, and top founders like Sam Altman.

Our mission

To make the best educational talking head videos on the planet. Our clients have ideas that can genuinely change people’s lives. Our job is to make those ideas impossible to scroll past, without ever trading substance for noise.

The role

A great video nobody clicks is a wasted video. You’ll own packaging across every client channel: the thumbnail, the title and how they connect to the first seconds of the video. You’ll design most of it yourself, and you’ll teach the team what good packaging looks like and push back when we’re about to ship something weak.

  1. Concept boards

    For every video, build a board with your read on the video, title options, outlier research and concept directions, all in one place, so a strategist can pick with confidence.

  2. Variations, without being asked

    Big text or small? Smiling or serious? Studio or real location? Don’t ask, export both. Strong boards carry 15 to 50 variations. The client should feel like we tried everything.

  3. Thumbnail, title and hook as one idea

    The thumbnail makes a promise, the title completes it and the first seconds of the video pay it off. You’ll make sure all three tell the same story, and suggest titles, not just pixels.

  4. Data and testing

    You’ll have access to client YouTube Studio accounts. Read impressions and click-through rate, spot videos worth repackaging, and run tests while videos are fresh, when YouTube responds fastest.

  5. Raise the bar

    Educate strategists and editors on what makes packaging work. Bring references, new patterns and honest opinions to every review.

  6. Your own rhythm

    Plan your day each morning, share what’s done each evening, and turn work around in 24 hours. You set your capacity, and then you hit it.

Ideal candidate

  • A portfolio of YouTube thumbnails with the results to back them up.
  • Able to explain why a thumbnail works, not just that it does.
  • Knows where to find references, and how to use them without copying.
  • Fluent in Photoshop, and comfortable using AI tools to explore ideas faster.
  • Understands click-through rate, A/B testing and when a test isn’t worth running.
  • Communicates more under pressure, not less, and flags problems early.
  • Keeps learning, and can name who they study and what they changed their mind about recently.

Bonus points if you

  • Have designed for top YouTube channels.
  • Have managed or coached other designers.
  • Can turn a thumbnail into other formats: carousels, banners and community posts.

How we approach packaging

  1. Study the outliers

    Find what’s overperforming in and around the niche, and spot the themes.

  2. Understand why they work

    The emotion, the promise, the composition, the words. Be able to say it out loud.

  3. Do it better

    Copying the outlier is step one. Beating it is the job.

What success looks like

  • Strategists pick from boards with confidence
  • Click-through rate trends up across channels
  • Every board shows real range
  • Work turned around in 24 hours
  • The team gets better at packaging
  • Clear, early communication

What you can expect

A small, ambitious team that moves fast. You’ll have real ownership: hit your deadlines and you’ll have the freedom to work the way that suits you best. In busy seasons that can mean the occasional evening or weekend to get a launch over the line.

This is a role you can grow: as the client roster grows, so does the packaging team, and you’ll be the one who builds it.

Benefits

  • Work from anywhere. We’re a fully remote team.
  • Top 1% pay for your location and experience, paid consistently every month.
  • Generous time off. 25 days of holiday, plus the Christmas break off.
  • Team retreat. In 2027 we’re bringing the whole team together in person.
  • Impact. Your thumbnails will be seen by millions of people around the world.
  • Great clients. Work directly with some of the most respected experts and founders on YouTube.

This probably isn’t for you if

  • You need a detailed brief before you start.
  • You deliver one design and wait for feedback.
  • Your best work is loud clickbait. Our clients are experts, and the packaging has to earn trust.
  • You’d rather be told what to make than argue for what will work.

How to apply

Send us your thumbnail portfolio, plus a short note on:

  • Three thumbnails you’re proudest of, and the results if you know them.
  • Where you get your references, and how you keep learning.
  • Your availability and time zone.

A short Loom walking us through one of your thumbnails puts you ahead of most applicants. Shortlisted designers do a paid test task and a call with Saf. Successful hires start on a paid, full-time probation, with a check-in at 30 to 45 days.

Where you’ll be

Fully remote. Our team works across the UK, India, Estonia and Brazil. You’ll need a few hours of overlap with UK afternoons for reviews and feedback.
