Our Core Values

Not posters on a wall. How we actually work, every day.

  1. 01

    Excellence

    We’re in the pursuit of greatness. We’re never satisfied with where we are, because there’s a better version of ourselves to pursue tomorrow. We’re all A-players, so someone else’s excellence never intimidates us. We do it for the love of the game.

  2. 02

    Radical Ownership

    If something goes wrong, we look at ourselves first. We own outcomes, not tasks, and we own our growth. Candid feedback comes naturally: we can take as good as we give, because the best idea wins. Full trust, full autonomy.

  3. 03

    Urgency

    We’re comfortable with ruthless reprioritisation, and we move quickly to execute. We question every timeline. AI takes the busywork off our plate. Our advantage is that we’re small: we finish things before bigger teams ever get sign-off.

  4. 04

    Overcommunication

    We’re remote-first across every time zone, so precise language matters. If there’s ambiguity or a hidden assumption, we send the message. We celebrate the people who record a Loom so everyone has exactly what they need. Responsive and proactive, never perfectionist and reactive.

  5. 05

    Seek the Win-Win

    A rising tide lifts all boats. We always give more than we take, with our teammates, partners and clients. If someone’s winning at another’s expense, we change the shape of things until everyone wins. Generous, and respectful of boundaries.
