About Talking Heads

Talking Heads is a done-for-you YouTube team for established founders and experts. We handle everything from strategy, titles and thumbnails to editing, animation and A/B testing.

We work with channels like Ali Abdaal, Daniel Priestley and Vishen Lakhiani. Our founder, Saf, was previously Creative Director for Ali Abdaal.

The business is already oversubscribed from referrals alone.

Our dream roster includes top creators like Colin and Samir, top companies like Formula 1, and top founders like Sam Altman.

Our mission

To make the best educational talking head videos on the planet. Our clients have ideas that can genuinely change people’s lives. Our job is to make those ideas impossible to scroll past, without ever trading substance for noise.

The role

This is not a cut-and-trim role. We call our editors video storytellers. You’ll bring real editorial judgement: story, hooks, pacing and taste, and the ability to make a video feel tight, modern and premium. As a senior editor, you’ll also set the standard for the rest of the editing team.

  1. A client channel

    Each editor is dedicated to one client. You’ll know their voice, their audience and their style better than anyone.

  2. Long form story edits

    Around four 20 to 30 minute videos a month. Turn raw footage into a clean, compelling story with a strong hook, tight structure and no dead air.

  3. Custom motion

    Build custom After Effects visuals: titles, charts, UI callouts and tasteful overlays that support what’s being said without distracting from it.

  4. Repeatable assets

    Use our pre-built assets to move fast, and build reusable pre-comps of your own, while keeping every video feeling fresh.

  5. Feedback loops

    Iterate through Frame.io and Slack. Each version should be meaningfully better than the last.

  6. Raise the bar

    Help define our editing standards, give feedback to other editors and share what works, so the whole team gets better.

Ideal candidate

  • Expert level in Premiere Pro and After Effects.
  • Proven experience making long form YouTube videos that performed. Talking head and educational content is a big plus.
  • Able to handle complex edits: robust motion graphics, advanced pacing and storytelling techniques.
  • Experience on high-visibility clients, with a strong feel for quality standards and brand voice.
  • Deep knowledge of visual trends on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and the ability to reverse engineer them.
  • A naturally strong aesthetic: clean, high contrast, modern.
  • Able to mentor other editors through feedback and best practice sharing.
  • Extremely deadline-driven, and calm when priorities change fast.

Bonus points if you

  • Have edited for top YouTube channels.
  • Are strong at sound design: mixing, music ducking, layered sound effects.
  • Can take a reference or a light brief and still elevate it with taste.

The style we’re after

You’ll need to be able to make videos like these:

  • Daniel Priestley: How I Made $10 Million From Appearing on Diary of a CEO
  • Daniel Priestley: The $1 Million Landing Page (Works For Normal People)
  • Daniel Priestley: The Entrepreneur Mindset: How to Become Obsessed
  • Ali Abdaal: 3 Strategies for Sustainable Success (short form)

What success looks like

  • The edit feels effortless to watch
  • Tight structure, great rhythm, no dead air
  • Motion is tasteful and intentional
  • Eased keyframes and clean typography
  • Deadlines hit, every time
  • Clear, early communication

Your setup

An Apple silicon Mac with enough memory for 30 minute timelines, internet of around 100 Mbps or faster, and your own Adobe subscription.

What you can expect

A small, ambitious team that moves fast. You’ll have real ownership: hit your deadlines and you’ll have the freedom to work the way that suits you best. In busy seasons that can mean the occasional evening or weekend to get a video over the line.

You’ll play a key part in raising the quality bar of the editing team, and we’ll invest in your growth: regular feedback, honest conversations about where you want to go, and room to grow into a lead editor role.

Benefits

  • Hybrid working. 2 days a week together in London, near Waterloo, and remote the other 3.
  • Great pay. £40,000 a year, paid consistently every month.
  • Generous time off. 25 days of holiday, plus the Christmas break off.
  • Team retreat. In 2027 we’re bringing the whole team together in person.
  • Impact. Your work will be seen by hundreds of thousands of people around the world.
  • Great clients. Work directly with some of the most respected experts and founders on YouTube.

This probably isn’t for you if

  • Your style is loud and sensationalist. Our clients are experts, and the edit has to earn trust.
  • You’re a perfectionist who can’t ship when the deadline comes.
  • You go quiet when you’re behind.
  • You need a detailed brief for every decision.

How to apply

Send us one or two of your best talking head, educational YouTube videos, plus a short note on:

  • Your role in those edits: editing, motion, design or sound.
  • Your typical workflow between Premiere and After Effects.
  • Your availability and time zone.

A short Loom on why you’d be a great fit puts you ahead of most applicants. Shortlisted editors do a paid test task and a call with Saf. Successful hires start on a paid, full-time probation, with a check-in at 30 to 45 days.

Where you’ll be

Hybrid: 2 days a week in person in London, near Waterloo, and remote the rest of the week. The wider team works across the UK, India, Estonia and Brazil.

Sound like you?

Applying takes a few minutes. Show us your best work and we’ll be in touch.
